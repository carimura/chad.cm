I’m not sure who needs to hear any of this but...

Whatever you’re going through, whatever confusion, valleys, or darkness you may be facing, Jesus loves you. He will meet you where you are and then transform you. Turn to Him. Repent and believe. That’s it.

I did. And Jesus saved me. He lived a perfect life, died on the cross for my sins, rose from the dead, ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of the Father.

By His Grace, I survived my 20s mostly unscathed when I probably shouldn’t have.

Through His providence, I met my wife in my 30s, she stayed with me, and we have three amazing children.

Because of His blessings, the Lord has provided over a nearly 30-year career in tech.

It hasn’t been perfect. We’ve both lost parents way too soon, at times feel like we’re wandering in the desert, have our own struggles, and life will certainly bring new challenges.

But through it all, Jesus is at the center.

I believe the Lord is leading me in a much different direction for the next decade. More on that soon if you haven’t unfollowed me already.

> “And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ.”
>
> — Colossians 3:23–24
